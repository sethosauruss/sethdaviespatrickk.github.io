Background

I'm studying a Bachelor of Information Technology at La Trobe University, with a focus on software engineering, networking, and cybersecurity. Outside of study, I work in retail and customer-facing roles, which is where a lot of my troubleshooting habits actually come from — diagnosing problems for people who can't describe them technically.

I also do freelance graphic design work on the side, which occasionally crosses over — clean documentation and diagrams matter as much as the systems behind them.

U2

Why the home lab

Coursework covers concepts; a home lab is where I actually break things and have to work out why. It started as a single Raspberry Pi running a few self-hosted services, and it's currently being rebuilt into a small rack-mounted environment with proper network segmentation, a dedicated firewall, and a small SIEM setup — closer to what a real small-business network actually looks like.

Outside of IT

3D printing and general electronics tinkering, mostly in service of the lab itself — I design and print my own rack mounts rather than buying off-the-shelf brackets where I can help it.
